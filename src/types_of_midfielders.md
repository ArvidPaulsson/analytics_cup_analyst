
## Types of defensive midfielders 

### Anchor Man / Makélélé Role
Stays deep, breaks up opposition attacks, and provides stability.
Rarely leaves position, focusing on protecting the back line and maintaining balance.

Key strengths: Tackling, positioning, defensive awareness, discipline.

Examples: Claude Makélélé, Casemiro, Fabinho.

### Regista
A deep-lying playmaker who dictates the tempo of the game and orchestrates build-up play from deep areas.
Thrives under pressure, using vision and passing range to control possession.

Key strengths: Passing, composure, vision, tactical intelligence.

Examples: Sergio Busquets, Jorginho, Andrea Pirlo.

### Box-to-Box 6
A more dynamic midfielder who contributes both defensively and offensively.
Covers large spaces, supports attacks, and recovers quickly to defend transitions.

Key strengths: Stamina, ball carrying, pressing, versatility.

## Types of central midfielders

### Box-to-Box Midfielder
Covers the entire pitch, contributing both defensively and offensively.
Supports the defence, breaks lines with forward runs, and adds energy to transitions.

Key strengths: Stamina, tackling, timing of runs, work rate.

Examples: Federico Valverde, Nicolò Barella, Jude Bellingham.

### Deep-Lying Playmaker
Controls the tempo of the game from deeper areas.
Uses precise passing and vision to progress play, often switching the ball to exploit space.

Key strengths: Passing range, composure, vision, tactical awareness.

Examples: Toni Kroos, Thiago Alcântara, Frenkie de Jong.

### Mezzala (Attacking 8)
An advanced No. 8 who operates in the half-spaces and final third.
Combines creativity with intelligent movement, linking midfield and attack.

Key strengths: Ball control, chance creation, movement between lines, combination play.

Examples: Enzo Fernández, Bernardo Silva, Ilkay Gündogan

## Types of attacking midfielders 

### Classic No.10
A central creative playmaker who dictates attacking play.
Finds pockets of space, plays decisive passes, and orchestrates the final third.

Key strengths: Vision, passing, creativity, composure in tight spaces.

Examples: Mesut Özil, Kevin De Bruyne, Zinedine Zidane.

### Second Striker / Shadow Striker
Plays closer to the main forward, focusing on movement and goal threat rather than pure playmaking.
Makes late runs into the box, exploits space around the striker, and looks to finish chances.

Key strengths: Positioning, finishing, timing of runs, link-up play.

Examples: Marek Hamšík, Dele Alli, Jude Bellingham.

### Raumdeuter (Space Interpreter)
Specialises in intelligent off-ball movement to exploit gaps in the opponent’s defence.
Not heavily involved in build-up but consistently finds dangerous positions to score or assist.

Key strengths: Spatial awareness, movement, anticipation, finishing.

Best example: Thomas Müller.
